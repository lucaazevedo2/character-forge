# Wild Shape sources and scope

- Rules and creature statistics: D&D SRD 5.1, 2014 rules, CC BY 4.0. Existing site attribution and license notices apply. Form statistics were retrieved from `https://www.dnd5eapi.co/api/2014/monsters/{index}` on October 30, 2026 and are bundled locally, without runtime requests.
- Recommendations: RPGBOT, Practical Guide to DnD 5e Wild Shape, updated October 7, 2026, https://rpgbot.net/dnd5/characters/classes/druid/wild-shape/. The page could not be fetched directly; Luca supplied its text in this conversation. Short, paraphrased role notes were derived from that text; color ratings were not copied.
- 88 stat blocks are included: 84 beast forms and four named Moon elemental forms. These are not every published beast; locked high-CR forms are shown only with Show locked forms enabled.
- The picker applies 2014 druid level limits: ordinary CR 1/4, 1/2 at level 4, 1 at level 8; Moon CR 1 at level 2 and floor(druid level/3) from level 6; swimming at level 4 and flying at level 8. Moon elementals unlock at level 10 and use two charges. Duration is half druid level in hours, rounded down, advanced manually with game time.
- The original character remains the source of normal HP, mental ability scores, class resources, spell choices, and equipment. A saved `wildShape` state owns form HP and duration; stat functions overlay the form without replacing the hero’s base scores. Ending at 0 applies overflow once; the pulsating button acknowledges the already-ended form and restores the normal sheet.
- Gear defaults to merged/inactive. Unarmored Defense is an alternative AC calculation, not an addition to natural armor. Other body-dependent retained features require DM judgment; the form AC adjustment supports approved corrections. Damage is entered after applying resistance/immunity. Recharge and conditional action availability remain manual, with full source descriptions displayed.
